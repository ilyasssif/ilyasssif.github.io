About Ilyass Sif

Ilyass Sif is an artificial intelligence researcher and software engineer studying Computer Engineering at École Nationale des Sciences Appliquées de Tanger (ENSA Tanger).

His research and engineering focus on high-throughput, hardware-accelerated reinforcement learning (RL) simulation environments using JAX (`vmap`, `jit`), distributed multi-agent systems, and formal mathematical optimization principles.

He is the creator of HaxGym, a GPU-accelerated simulation framework designed to train multi-agent policies millions of steps per second on consumer hardware.
